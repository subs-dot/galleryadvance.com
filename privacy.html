Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gallery Advance ("Gallery Advance," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website or apply for a merchant cash advance.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: business name, industry, state, time in business, revenue ranges, funding needs, and your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Documents you share later: bank statements, identification, and other materials requested during underwriting.
  • Technical information: when you load our pages, our web host and our font provider (Google Fonts) automatically receive standard request data such as your IP address and browser type. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Information from third parties: business and personal credit reports, public records, and identity-verification services, used to evaluate your application.

How we use information

  • To review your application and present funding offers
  • To contact you about your application and account
  • To verify identity and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our website and services

How we share information

We share information with funding partners and service providers who help us evaluate applications, provide funding, and operate our business; with credit bureaus and verification services; and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages and calls

If you opt in, we may contact you by call or text about funding offers. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Cookies

Our website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy.

Data security and retention

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
